# Wamiro — Getting Started

Wamiro is your company's single digital workplace: one login for people,
work, requests, knowledge, documents, support, analytics and AI.

## For everyone

1. **Sign in** with the email your administrator invited. Set a strong password.
2. **Secure your account**: Settings → Security → enable MFA (TOTP authenticator app).
3. **Find your way**: the left rail switches major areas (Home, People, Work,
   Requests, Knowledge, Documents, Support, Analytics, AI, Admin). The sidebar
   under each area lists its features. `Ctrl/Cmd+K` opens global search.
4. **Notifications** appear in the top bar; unread counts update as work
   happens.

## For managers

- Your **team view** shows direct reports (People → Team).
- **Approvals**: Requests → Approvals shows what waits on you. Delegates see
  them too when a delegator is away.
- **Analytics**: dashboards respect your scope — you see your team, not the
  whole company.

## For administrators

Start with the setup checklist on the Admin home page:

1. Organization details (`/settings/organization`)
2. Departments (`/admin` → departments)
3. Roles & permissions (`/admin/roles`)
4. Users & invitations (`/admin/users`)
5. Modules (`/settings/organization`) — disabled modules disappear from navigation
6. Security center (`/admin/security`) — sessions, MFA adoption
7. Integrations (`/admin` → email, SSO/SCIM if used)

Every administrative action lands in `/admin/audit`.

## Where to get help

- In-product knowledge base (Knowledge workspace)
- Support tickets (Support workspace) — priority and SLA apply
- Your company administrators
